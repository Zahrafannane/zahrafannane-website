Card 01Awareness
The Pattern Interrupt
Your brain replays familiar patterns automatically to save energy. It cannot tell the difference between a pattern that helps you and one that doesn't. Noticing the pattern is the first interruption.
Daily Practice: Every morning this week, write one pattern you noticed yourself in the day before. Patterns become visible through repetition.

Card 12Belief
The Belief Inventory
Most beliefs running your life were formed before age 7 — by a child making sense of the world with limited information. They were never meant to be permanent.
Daily Practice: Each morning this week, finish: I am someone who ___. On day 7, mark which ones you chose and which ones chose you.

Card 29Identity
The Permission Slip
Identity does not follow achievement — achievement follows identity. You become first, then the evidence appears. Most people are waiting for permission to become who they already are.
Daily Practice: Each morning write: I give myself permission to be someone who ___. Write something new every day for 30 days.
ZF ✦
Card 40Safety
The Breath Reset
Slow breathing activates the parasympathetic nervous system — the body's built-in off switch for stress. A 5-second inhale and 7-second exhale shifts you from threat to calm in under 2 minutes.
Daily Practice: Practice every morning before checking your phone. 4 rounds. Over 21 days it becomes the nervous system's new baseline.
ZF ✦
Card 53Action
The Identity Vote
Every action you take is a vote for who you are becoming. You do not need a perfect day. You need one vote — then another. The votes accumulate into identity.
Daily Practice: Each evening write: the vote I cast for my future self today was ___. Do this every night for 30 days.
